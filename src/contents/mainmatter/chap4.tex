\chapter{Klein Gordon Theory}\label{ch:KG-theory}
        \section{Introduction}
        In order to get a sensible description of the quantum Klein--Gordon field, we shall follow a procedure similar to the one we use when we quantize classical systems. In standard Quantum Mechanics we start from the generalized coordinates and momenta in the phase space $\pqty{\tensor{q}{^\alpha}, \tensor{p}{_\alpha}}$, with their Canonical Commutation Relations, defined in terms of Poisson brackets, and promote them to operators, defined by analogous Commutation Relations for the operators:
        \begin{align*}
                \Large\{\tensor{q}{^\alpha},\tensor{q}{^\beta}\Large\} = \poissonbracket{\tensor{p}{_\alpha}}{\tensor{p}{_\beta}} = 0 & \rightarrow ciao\\
                \poissonbracket{\tensor{q}{^\alpha}}{\tensor{p}{_\beta}} &= \tensor{\delta}{^\alpha_\beta}
        \end{align*}
